### Hi, I'm Thomas

I build tools for AI that runs where the data lives: local models, your own
knowledge, open code. Most of it starts as something I need myself and lands
here once it is solid enough to share.

**Inntal-KI** ([inntal-ki.eu](https://inntal-ki.eu)) is my personal,
non-commercial project about AI that runs on your own hardware — the model is
a file, not a service.

#### Projects

- **[obsidian-intelligence](https://github.com/GuideThomas/obsidian-intelligence)** —
  turns an Obsidian vault into an MCP server: graph analysis, full-text and
  semantic search, without uploading a single note. `npx obsidian-intelligence`
- **[loomwerk](https://github.com/GuideThomas/loomwerk)** — an experiment: an
  innovation engine that breeds ideas from tensions in your own knowledge
  sources, culls most of them on purpose and matures the survivors.
- **[dsgvo-ki-checkliste](https://github.com/inntal-ki/dsgvo-ki-checkliste)** —
  GDPR and EU AI Act self-assessment before introducing AI (German).
- **[open-webui-starter-de](https://github.com/inntal-ki/open-webui-starter-de)** —
  Open WebUI + Ollama starter with German defaults, pinned versions,
  local-only by default (German).
